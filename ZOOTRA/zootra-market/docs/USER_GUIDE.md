## Introduction

Zootra Market is a platform designed for farmers and pet owners to buy and sell products and services related to livestock, feed, pets, and animal health. This guide will walk you through the essential features and functionalities of the platform.

## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

1. Navigate to the **Register** page.
2. Fill in the required fields: name, email, phone number, and password.
3. Click on the **Register** button to create your account.

### Logging In

1. Go to the **Login** page.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Click on the **Login** button to access your dashboard.

## Using the Public Dashboard

### My Listings

- View all your product listings.
- Edit or delete existing listings.
- Mark listings as sold.

### Creating a Listing

1. Click on the **Create Listing** button.
2. Select a category (Livestock, Feed, Pet, Health).
3. Upload images, set a price, and add a description.
4. Click on the **Publish** button to make your listing live.

### Managing Listings

- Edit your listings by clicking on the **Edit** button next to each listing.
- Delete listings you no longer want to display.
- View inquiries from potential buyers.

### My Bookings

- View all your bookings for services.
- Cancel or reschedule bookings as needed.

### WhatsApp Integration

- Connect with sellers via WhatsApp.
- Use pre-filled message templates for quick communication.

### Becoming a Service Provider

1. Navigate to the **Become a Service Provider** section.
2. Fill out the application form, including your service type and availability.
3. Upload any required documents (licenses, certificates).
4. Submit your application for admin verification.

## Admin Dashboard

### Admin Login

- Admins can log in using the **Admin Login** page.
- Enter your admin credentials to access the admin dashboard.

### Managing Listings

- Approve or reject product listings submitted by users.
- Delete listings that violate platform policies.

### Managing Users

- View all registered users and their roles.
- Suspend or activate user accounts as necessary.
- Change user roles or delete accounts.

### Analytics and Reports

- Access analytics on user activity, listings, and bookings.
- Generate reports for insights into platform performance.
